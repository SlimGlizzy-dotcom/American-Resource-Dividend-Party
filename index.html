Building a Future for All Americans

The American Resource Dividend Party believes America’s natural wealth should benefit every citizen. By responsibly managing national resources, we can provide healthcare, education, infrastructure, and economic security for the American people.

“America is rich. The people deserve a share.”

10 Point Platform

Explore our platform and learn more about the principles guiding the American Resource Dividend Party. Select any card to read more.

1. Resource Ownership

America’s natural resources should benefit the people directly.

Learn more

We support public-interest management of national natural resources, with transparent oversight and policies designed to ensure the public shares in the wealth those resources create.

2. Basic Income

Create a national dividend funded through resource profits.

Learn more

Develop a public resource dividend that distributes a share of eligible resource revenues to Americans, subject to sustainable funding and transparent financial reporting.

3. Universal Healthcare

Guarantee healthcare access for all Americans.

Learn more

Work toward a healthcare system that makes essential medical services accessible and affordable, reducing the financial burden of illness on individuals and families.

4. Free Education

Tuition-free colleges, trade schools, and public education support.

Learn more

Expand access to public education, vocational training, apprenticeships, and higher education so financial hardship does not prevent people from building valuable skills.

5. Fair Economy

Strengthen workers, families, and small businesses nationwide.

Learn more

Support fair wages, strong local businesses, worker protections, and economic opportunities that allow families to build stable and productive lives.

6. Energy Independence

Invest in domestic energy and renewable innovation.

Learn more

Encourage reliable domestic energy production, renewable technologies, research, and infrastructure while balancing affordability, environmental responsibility, and energy security.

7. Anti-Corruption

Remove corporate influence from public resource management.

Learn more

Strengthen conflict-of-interest rules, public disclosure, independent audits, and accountability measures to help ensure resource decisions serve the public interest.

8. Housing Support

Expand affordable housing and stronger communities.

Learn more

Promote affordable housing, responsible community development, and policies that help individuals and families access safe, stable places to live.

9. Infrastructure

Modernize roads, utilities, internet, and transportation systems.

Learn more

Invest in dependable public infrastructure, including transportation, water systems, utilities, and broadband, to strengthen communities and support long-term economic growth.

10. Transparency

Ensure public accountability and open financial reporting.

Learn more

Publish clear financial reports, disclose how resource revenues are collected and spent, and establish meaningful oversight so the public can evaluate government performance.

Our Mission

We believe America’s natural wealth belongs to the people. Our mission is to transform resource profits into opportunity, stability, healthcare, education, and prosperity for every citizen.
